## Triacylglycerol Digestion
Occurs at two locations
- Mouth—lingual lipase
- Stomach—gastric lipase

Does not require other protein cofactors for maximal activity

## Reverse Cholesterol Transport Defined
Removal of cholesterol from the circulation and peripheral tissues to the liver
Plasma HDL levels are increased.
## Apolipoprotein E: A Brain Lipid
Because of the blood–brain barrier, the brain must make its own cholesterol.
Neurons secrete small, disk-shaped, HDL-like particles coated with apolipoprotein E (apoE).
ApoE is a brain lipid protein with multiple isoforms.
ApoE4 has been correlated to early onset Alzheimer’s disease.
## Carrier Protein Binding and Lipid Transport
Albumin
- Secreted by the liver
- Binds to nonspecific fatty acids through hydrophobic interactions
- Binding decreases drug efficacy and increases half-life
Transcortin
- Binds to several sex hormones, including cortisone, but also to aldosterone and progesterone
- Blocks the effect of signaling molecules

## Receptor-Mediated Endocytosis Defined
Process in which the cell internalizes particles and uses the cholesterol contents of low-density lipoproteins.
Ligand (clathrin) binds to the receptor.
Receptors begin to cluster in groups (patches) and form a coated pit, which in turn forms a coated vesicle.
Clathrin and adaptor proteins are removed and recycled to form new coated pits.

## Triacylglycerol Synthesis
Occurs in the liver and adipose tissue
Liver—de novo synthesis from actyl-CoA
Adipose tissue—re-esterifies free fatty acids (storage)
Enzymes are found in the endoplasmic reticulum.

## Lipin: Phosphatidate Phosphatase
Enzyme that catalyzes the dephosphorylation of phosphatidate to diacylglycerol
Helps coordinate neutral lipid synthesis with the biosynthesis of phosphatidylcholine and phosphatidyl ethanolamine
Forms a complex with several transcription factors that aids in regulating genes involved in fatty acid biosynthesis and beta-oxidation
## Section 10.6 Learning Objective

## Lipid Storage Droplets Defined
The organelle that stores neutral lipids in the cell
Contain a phospholipid monolayer coating a core of hydrophobic triacylglycerols or cholesterol esters
These coating core proteins are involved in the trafficking and metabolism of the fatty contents
Perilipin proteins are key in regulating lipid metabolism.

## Lipolysis Defined
The regulated mobilization and breakdown of triacylglycerols
Triggered by hormone signaling
Occurs at low background levels
Regulated by AMP kinase

## Triacylglycerol Storage
Acute storage
- Mobilization occurs over minutes to hours
- cAMP is manipulated via hormones and cell signaling cascades
Chronic storage
- Occurs over weeks or longer
- Partially affected by both levels of gene expression and diet
## Lipid Rafts Defined
Transient, loosely bound, bundles of cholesterol and sphingolipids (especially sphingomyelin) thought to be involved in signaling
Cholesterol is essential to the raft and depletion or manipulation disrupts the raft.
Shape of molecules facilitates tight packing.
Interact with proteins in cell signaling through GPI anchors
Sites of action for pathogen entry into a cell and cell survival.
